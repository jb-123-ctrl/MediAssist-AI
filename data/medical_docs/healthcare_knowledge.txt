Fever

Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature.

Common causes:
- Viral infections
- Bacterial infections
- Heat exhaustion
- Certain inflammatory conditions

General advice:
- Drink plenty of fluids.
- Rest adequately.
- Monitor body temperature.
- Seek medical attention if the fever is persistent, very high, or accompanied by severe symptoms.

Headache

Headaches may be caused by:
- Stress
- Dehydration
- Lack of sleep
- Eye strain
- Viral illness

General advice:
- Stay hydrated.
- Rest.
- Reduce screen time.
- Seek medical care if headaches are sudden, severe, or accompanied by neurological symptoms.

Nutrition

Healthy eating includes:
- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Whole grains
- Lean protein
- Adequate hydration
